## Requesting a service

Requests start from the services catalogue. Open the service you need, pick an option if it has any, and choose **Request Now**: the request is created and you land on its record, where the service asks only for what it actually needs — language pair and turnaround for a translation, structure and details for a company formation.

If nothing in the catalogue fits, use **Create Custom Request** and describe the need in your own words.

## Documents

Most services need paperwork before work can start: your ID, a certificate, an existing contract. The request tells you what is required and you upload it right there in the thread.

Each document carries a status — **pending review**, **approved**, **rejected** or **replaced** — and a rejection comes with feedback so you know what to fix. If you upload a newer version, the old one is kept as superseded rather than deleted. You can see required documents for a service before you request it, on the service page.

## The quote

Once your request is matched with a vetted professional, the quote appears in the thread: the total, the date payment is due, and a PDF you can keep for your records. Nothing starts before you pay, and the provider is only told to begin after payment is confirmed.

You can export the quote as a PDF, and download your own documents at any time.

## Paying

Two ways to pay, both inside the request — see [Payments](/docs/payments) for the detail:

- **Pay Now** — online card or wallet payment (local wallets and cards during early access; international cards coming).
- **Bank transfer** — transfer, then upload the receipt in the request. The Meridian team reviews it and confirms the payment; you will see *under review*, then *payment confirmed* or *proof rejected* with a reason.

A request is paid in one amount, the full approved quote. We do not take a deposit, a milestone or a part payment, and a quote is not a subscription.

Your request only moves to **work in progress** once the payment is confirmed — a return to the website is never treated as proof that money arrived.

## While the work happens

- **One thread** — messages, status updates, documents, quote and invoice all live in the request. You do not have to chase anyone by email.
- **Meetings** — when a call is needed, it is scheduled from the request and you get an invite with the join link by email and in your portal.
- **Status** — the request shows where it is: in review, action needed, in progress, completed or closed. The status follows the work, so you cannot set or change it yourself; "action needed" means it is waiting on you.

## When it is done

When your expert marks the work delivered, the request moves to **fulfilled** and then **completed**. You get an invoice in the thread and by email, and the payment details stay on the request permanently.

If something is wrong with a completed request, use **Report an Issue** in the thread. It is available once the request is completed, it takes a short description, and it notifies the Meridian team and the provider so the problem gets handled rather than sitting in your inbox.

## What we ask of you

- Answer the questions in the request — a request with missing information goes nowhere.
- Upload documents we ask for, in a readable form.
- Keep messages in the request thread so the history stays in one place.
- Tell us quickly if something goes wrong, rather than after the work has drifted for weeks.
