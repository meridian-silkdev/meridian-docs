## Requesting a service

Submit a project brief describing what you need. A provider reviews it and responds with a quote — scope, fixed price, and timeline.

## Reviewing and accepting a quote

Quotes are itemized and shown in your project panel. Once you accept a quote, the project moves to the payment phase.

## Paying

You can pay online (see [Payments](/docs/payments)) or by bank wire/check where the provider offers it. Payment can cover a deposit, a milestone, or the full amount, depending on how the quote is structured.

## Tracking delivery

Once payment is confirmed, the project moves to **in progress**. You can follow status updates and communicate with the provider directly from the project panel.
