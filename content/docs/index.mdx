Meridian gets a founder from "I need this done" to a verified professional actively working on it — in one place, in one thread, with the status, the documents and the money all tracked.

You are not hiring a law firm through us, and you are not filing forms with us. **Meridian is the marketplace, vetting and payment layer**: independent, licensed professionals do the regulated work, and we make sure they are who they say they are, that you see a real price before you commit, and that the work actually gets finished.

## Who it is for

Founders and startup operators who need the last mile of business work handled by someone accountable: company formation and legal files, visas and relocation, accounting and tax, business plans and finance, digitalization and AI, and document translation.

## What you can do here

- **Request a service** — pick it from the catalogue; what you are asked depends on the service.
- **Get a quote, then pay** — you see the price and the due date before you commit, and you pay inside the platform.
- **Track it to the end** — every step, message and document lives in the same thread.
- **Read guides** — short, platform-written explainers on the decisions founders actually face, free to read in your portal.
- **Bring your own agent** — connect an AI agent to your account so it can browse services, start a request and follow it, under the same rules you are held to. See [Using an AI agent](/docs/agents).

## How a request works

Every request moves through the same path, and you can see where it is at any time:

1. **Pick a service** — from the catalogue, or a custom request if nothing fits; the questions depend on the service.
2. **We review it and find your expert** — we check the request, then match it with a vetted professional for that work.
3. **Your quote** — scope, total and payment-due date, in your thread. Nothing starts until you pay.
4. **Work in progress** — messages, documents and status in the same place, all the way to delivery.

Full detail: [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).

## Where Meridian operates

Meridian is in early access and opening country by country, starting with Portugal, then Spain and France — [join early access](https://meridian.silkdev.com.tn/early-access) and we will tell you the moment your country and service are ready.

The platform itself runs in **English, French and Portuguese**. You can change language from the portal.

## What Meridian is not

Meridian is not a law firm, an accounting firm or an immigration agency, and we do not give legal, tax or professional advice. We route you to independent, licensed providers and hold the payment layer. A provider who is not right for the work is not on the platform.
