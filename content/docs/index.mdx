description: What Meridian is and who it's for.
---

Meridian connects **customers** who need a service done with **providers** who deliver it. A project moves through a shared lifecycle — intake, quote, payment, delivery — visible to both sides in one portal.

This documentation is split by role:

- **[For customers](/docs/for-customers)** — request a service, review a quote, pay, and track delivery.
- **[For providers](/docs/for-providers)** — receive requests, send quotes, and get paid.
- **[Payments](/docs/payments)** — how payment collection and payout works on the platform.

If you're new here, start with [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).
