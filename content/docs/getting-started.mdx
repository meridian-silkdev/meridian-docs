## Create an account

Sign up with your email address. Every new account is a **customer** account — you can request services, follow them, pay and message your provider from there.

If you are a professional who wants to deliver services on Meridian, that is a separate track: you [apply to join the provider network](/docs/for-providers) and we review and onboard you. One account has one role.

Once you are in, you land on your dashboard. Requests start from **New request** in the sidebar, which opens the services catalogue.

## Make your first request

1. **Pick a service** from the catalogue and choose **Request Now**. The service decides what you are asked, so pick the closest match. Nothing fits? Use **Create Custom Request**.
2. **Fill in the details** on the request record. They depend on the service: for a translation you choose your language pair and turnaround; for a company formation you give the structure and the details that go on the documents.
3. **Upload what the service requires.** Some services ask for documents up front — your ID, a certificate, an existing contract. The request tells you exactly what is needed, and you can re-upload if something is rejected.
4. **Submit.** Your request enters review.

## What happens next

| Step | What you see |
|---|---|
| Requirements | "Share the details we need to get started." — upload what is missing and submit. |
| Reviewing your request | "Our team is reviewing your request." |
| Finding your expert | "We're matching your request with the right expert." You see the assigned provider's name once they are matched. |
| Your quote | "Your personalized quote, ready for review." — total, payment-due date, and a PDF you can keep. |
| Payment | "We're verifying your payment." — we only move on once the payment is confirmed. |
| Work in progress | "Your expert is working on it." |
| Delivery | "Your service has been delivered." |
| Completed | "All done — thanks for confirming!" |

Your request list groups everything into five columns — **In Review**, **Action Needed**, **In Progress**, **Completed** and **Closed** — so anything waiting on you is obvious.

## While you wait

- **Messages** — everything about a request stays in its own thread. You do not have to chase anyone over email.
- **Meetings** — when a call is useful, a meeting is scheduled from the request and the invite lands in your inbox with the join link.
- **Documents** — upload, re-upload and see whether each one is pending review, approved or rejected, with feedback when something needs fixing.
- **Guides** — free explainers in the portal, under Guides, for the parts of the process you want to understand before you commit.
- **Notifications** — in-app plus email, for quotes, invoices, meetings and status changes. You choose which emails you get in Settings.
