## Create an account

Sign up on the Meridian client portal. Every account can act as a customer, a provider, or both — your role is decided per project, not per account.

## The project lifecycle

Every project moves through the same phases, visible to both the customer and the provider from the portal:

1. **Intake** — the customer describes what they need.
2. **Quote** — the provider proposes scope, price, and timeline.
3. **Payment** — the customer pays a deposit, milestone, or the full amount.
4. **In progress** — the provider delivers the work.
