### What is Meridian, exactly?

A marketplace and payment layer for business services. Independent, licensed professionals do the work — company formation, visas, accounting and tax, translation, digitalization — and Meridian vets them, routes requests to them, and handles quotes, payment and tracking. See the [overview](/docs/index).

### Are you a law firm or an accountant?

No. Meridian is not a law firm, an accounting firm or an immigration agency, and we do not give legal, tax or professional advice. Every provider on the platform is independent and licensed for the work they do, and we do not take on regulated work ourselves.

### Can one account be both a customer and a provider?

No — an account has one role. Signing up always creates a customer account. Professionals [apply to join the provider network](/docs/for-providers) and get a separate provider account after we review the application.

### I do not see my role I expected. Why?

Because signup is customer-only, on purpose. Provider accounts are created after a human review so that the people taking requests are the people we have checked.

### How are providers vetted?

Before a provider can take a request, we review their application and check who they are and what they are licensed to do. We also record how they perform — rating, response time, completion time — and use it when matching requests. If a provider turns out not to be right for the work, they do not stay on the platform.

### Do I get to choose my provider?

You see the name of the expert assigned to your request once the request is matched, and you can raise anything you are uncomfortable with at any point. What we do not do is pass a customer's private intake details around while providers bid — the matching, vetting and shortlist are our job, not yours.

### Do you take a deposit or split the payment?

No. A request is paid in one amount — the full approved quote. No deposit, no milestones, no part payment. See [Payments](/docs/payments).

### Can I pay by bank transfer?

Yes. Transfer using the details on your invoice, then upload the receipt in the request. A person at Meridian reviews it and confirms the payment. See [Payments](/docs/payments).

### How do I know a payment actually went through?

Every payment is checked directly with the payment provider before a request is marked paid. A redirect back to the site, or a receipt on its own, is never treated as proof. The status on your request is the source of truth — if it does not say paid, it is not paid.

### Do you store my card details?

No. Card payments are completed on Flouci's hosted page. Meridian never sees or stores card numbers.

### How are providers rated?

From finished requests: a rating, how quickly they respond, and how long they take to complete. Those numbers follow the provider and feed into who gets matched to a new request.

### Can I use an AI agent instead of doing this myself?

Yes — connect an agent to your account with an API key and it can browse services, start a request, follow status and check payment, with approval prompts for anything involving money. See [Using an AI agent](/docs/agents).

### What if the work is not what was quoted?

Use **Report an Issue** in the request once it is completed, or email us. Describe what went wrong and we handle it with the provider. See [Payments](/docs/payments) for the detail.

### What if I need a request cancelled?

Tell us in the request thread as early as possible — before the work starts is much easier than after. A request is cancelled by the provider or by the Meridian team; there is no cancel button in the customer portal.

### Which languages is Meridian in?

English, French and Portuguese. You can switch language from the portal.

### Where is Meridian available?

Meridian is in early access and opening country by country, starting with Portugal, then Spain and France — [join the list](https://meridian.silkdev.com.tn/early-access) and we will tell you when your country and service are ready.

### Is Meridian live yet?

Not fully. Meridian is in early access, and we are onboarding customers and providers country by country rather than pretending a marketplace is already full. We would rather tell you that plainly than dress it up.

### Who do I contact for help?

The request thread itself is the fastest route — it reaches the right person with the full history attached. For anything else: [contact@meridian.com](mailto:contact@meridian.com).
