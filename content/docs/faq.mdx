### Can one account be both a customer and a provider?

Yes. Your role is decided per project, not per account.

### What happens if I need to cancel a project?

Cancellation terms are set out in the accepted quote for that project. Contact the other party directly through the project panel as early as possible.

### How do I know a payment actually went through?

The platform re-verifies every payment directly with the payment provider before marking it paid — the project's payment status in your portal is the source of truth.

### Who do I contact for help?

Reach out to [contact@silkdev.com.tn](mailto:contact@silkdev.com.tn).
