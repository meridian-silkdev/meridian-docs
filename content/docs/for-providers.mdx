Meridian providers are independent, licensed professionals — lawyers, notaries, accountants, visa agents, translators and consultants. We do not employ you, we do not give you work outside a request, and we do not compete with you. We vet, match, and handle the customer side so you can do the work you are licensed to do.

## Joining the network

Sign-up creates a customer account, so the provider track starts with an application: [apply to join the provider network](https://meridian.silkdev.com.tn/providers) with your name, email, provider type, country and the services you offer.

We review every application and onboard the ones we can work with — you get a provider account and a login once you are in. A provider account is a separate account from a customer account; if you need both, ask us.

## Setting up your services

Once onboarded, **My Services** lets you choose the services you want to receive requests for and set your rate for each. That selection is what puts a request in front of you: a request only reaches you if it matches something you have switched on.

## Available requests

Requests you can offer on appear under **Available Requests**, with the request, the service, and who the customer is. For each one you can submit an offer — your price, plus a note if you want to explain your approach or availability — and you can edit it while it is under review. You see the outcome either way: chosen or not.

The customer's full conversation is not open to you while you are bidding. Once you are assigned, the request thread opens and you work from there.

## Once you are assigned

- **Accept the amount, send your own quote, or decline.** If the amount is not right, send a quote of your own — the customer is not shown it directly, and we come back to you on it. Declining returns the request to matching; it is not held against you.
- **Ask for what you need.** You can see the documents the customer has uploaded with their status, and add your own document requirements to the request.
- **Schedule a meeting** when the work needs a call. The meeting is created from the request with the invitees you choose, and the customer gets the join link by email and in their portal.
- **Keep the thread in one place.** Messages, documents, status and the invoice for a request all live in the request itself.

## Finishing the work

When the work is done, finalize the request. If you cannot deliver, you can cancel the request from the same place — do it early and say why, so the customer is not left waiting.

## Getting paid

The customer's payment is collected and confirmed on the platform before your delivery step starts. You are not chasing invoices, and you are not invoicing the customer directly — Meridian handles collection and confirmation.

Payout terms are agreed with the Meridian team when you are onboarded, so ask for them then rather than assuming them.

## Your record

Every finished request feeds your record: a rating, how fast you respond, and how long you take to complete. That is how we judge whether to match you to a new request, so finishing well and finishing on time is the thing that compounds.

## Notifications

In-app plus email for assignments, quotes, meetings and status changes. Email preferences are in Settings.
