## What you pay

The total on your quote is the total you are invoiced. There is no platform fee, no card surcharge and nothing added at checkout — the number you approve is the number you pay.

A request is paid **in one amount**: the full approved quote. We do not take a deposit, a milestone or a part payment, and a quote is not a subscription.

## Currency

Services are priced in **Tunisian Dinars (TND)**, and that is the currency your quote, your invoice and your payment are all in.

## Paying online

Use **Pay Now** in the request. You are taken to Flouci, the Tunisian payment gateway, to complete the payment with a local wallet or bank card. Card details are entered on Flouci's page — Meridian never sees or stores them.

When a payment completes, the platform checks it directly with Flouci before anything is marked as paid. A return to the site, a browser redirect, or a screenshot is never treated as proof of payment.

Once your payment is confirmed, an invoice is posted in the request thread and emailed to you, the work starts, and your request moves to **in progress**.

## Paying by bank transfer

You can also pay by bank transfer:

1. Transfer the full quoted amount using the details on your invoice.
2. Upload the receipt in the request — a screenshot or PDF — with an optional note such as a reference number.
3. The Meridian team reviews it. The request shows **under review**, then **payment confirmed** or **proof rejected** with a reason, and you can upload a new proof if needed.

We confirm the payment for you, and the request advances exactly as it would after an online payment.

## What we do not do

- We do not take a deposit or a partial payment on a request.
- We do not mark a request paid on the strength of a redirect, a receipt alone, or a customer's say-so — a human at Meridian reviews off-platform payment proofs.
- We do not add fees to your quote at checkout.

## If something is wrong

If you were charged for a request that was not delivered as quoted, use **Report an Issue** in the request once it is completed, or contact us at [contact@meridian.com](mailto:contact@meridian.com). Cancellations, credits and refunds are handled case by case by the Meridian team — tell us what happened and we will tell you where you stand.

## Paying from outside Tunisia

Online payment today runs on Flouci and covers Tunisian wallets and cards in TND. International card payments and other currencies are being enabled as we open each country — [join early access](https://meridian.silkdev.com.tn/early-access) and we will tell you when yours is live.
