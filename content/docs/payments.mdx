---
title: Payments
description: How online and manual payments work on Meridian.
---

## Currency

All amounts are quoted and paid in Tunisian Dinars (TND).

## Online payments

Online payments are processed by **Flouci**, a Central Bank of Tunisia–licensed payment provider. At checkout you can pay by wallet or bank card. Meridian never sees or stores your card details.

Once a payment completes, the platform verifies it directly with Flouci before marking it paid — a redirect back to the portal alone is never treated as proof of payment.

## Bank wire or check

Where a provider offers it, you can also pay by bank wire transfer or check. These payments are recorded manually: once the provider confirms receipt, they mark the payment as paid from their admin panel and the project advances the same way an online payment would.

## Partial payments

A project's total may be split into a deposit, milestones, or paid in full — however the quote is structured. Each payment is tracked individually, and the project's overall payment status (unpaid, partial, paid) reflects the sum against the quoted total.
